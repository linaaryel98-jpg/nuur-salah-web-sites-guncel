You can delete your Nuur Salah account yourself at any time, from within the app. You do not need to contact us to do so.

## How to delete your account

1. Open the Nuur Salah app.
2. Go to the **Profile** tab.
3. In the **Account and privacy** section, tap **Delete my account**.
4. Confirm in the dialog that appears.

Deletion begins once you confirm and cannot be undone.

## What is deleted

- Your account details (name, email address and sign-in information)
- App data and preferences stored with your account

Where the law requires certain records to be kept, they are retained only until the relevant period ends. Details are in the [privacy policy]({{privacyUrl}}).

## Your subscription must be cancelled separately

The Premium subscription is managed through Apple. Deleting your account does not end it automatically. If you do not want it to renew, cancel it on your iPhone under **Settings › [Your Name] › Subscriptions › Nuur Salah**.

## Why Sign in with Apple users are asked to verify again

If you created your account with Sign in with Apple, you will be asked to sign in with your Apple ID once more during deletion. There are two reasons:

- To confirm that the request really comes from the account holder.
- To revoke the connection between the app and your Apple account, as Apple requires. Revoking it needs a fresh authorisation from Apple.

Once the connection is revoked, Nuur Salah is also removed from the **Sign in with Apple** list of your Apple ID.

## If something goes wrong
